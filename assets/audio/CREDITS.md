# Audio sources

`galo.mp3` and `coruja.ogg` are CC0 (public domain dedication); credit is optional.

- `galo.mp3`: "Rooster crow" by jsbarrett, HQ preview, unedited.
  https://freesound.org/people/jsbarrett/sounds/200339/
- `coruja.ogg`: "Owl Screeches" by Yacob_l, one barn owl screech cut from 12.0 s to
  15.0 s of the HQ preview, faded and loudness-normalized.
  https://freesound.org/people/Yacob_l/sounds/397690/
- `corinho_de_fogo.ogg`: background music, a *corinho de fogo* excerpt cut from 1:16
  to 1:38 (22 s) and imported as a loop. Supplied by the team; its original source
  and license are not recorded yet.
